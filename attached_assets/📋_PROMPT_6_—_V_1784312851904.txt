PROMPT 6: Events & Actor Events

Implement two event systems:

COMPANY EVENTS (random, every 1-3 weeks):
- Bad review on latest production (apologize or ignore)
- Brand sponsorship (+$2000)
- Competitor hit (-5 rep)
- Script leak (investigate or ignore)
- International platform offer (+$2500)
- Worldwide star approaches YOU (rare, rank ≤15)
- Fixed CP contract offer (if high chemistry pair exists)

ACTOR EVENTS (10 types):
1. Poached by rival agency (match offer or let go)
2. Scandal! (PR cleanup, ignore, or fire)
3. Off-script romance with non-CP co-star (confirm, deny, ignore)
4. Solo ambitions (allow or deny)
5. On-set injury (pause 2 weeks or replace)
6. Fan meeting request (organize or decline)
7. Raise demand (pay or refuse → may quit)
8. Unexpected chemistry viral moment (form CP or ignore)
9. Language barrier with international guest (tutor or risk quality)
10. Singing debut request (produce single or decline)

CHEMISTRY PULSE (weekly check):
- <25 chemistry: rumors or awkward interview (6 week cooldown)
- ≥75 chemistry: CP endorsement +$1200 (8 week cooldown)
- Fixed CP with <25 chemistry: breakup event
- Actor happiness <20 + idle 4+ weeks: quit threat
- Negative money: bankruptcy event (emergency loan or sell equipment)

MOBILE: Modal choices are full-width stacked buttons. Text is readable at small sizes. Scrollable modals for long content.
