PROMPT 4: Dashboard & Production System

Dashboard view:
- Active Productions card: shows filming progress bar, episode countdown, lead actor mini-portraits, combo status (hidden until wrap), last episode stats if releasing
- Completed Productions card: last 6 finished shows with star rating, revenue, genre
- Event Log card: scrollable, newest first, color-coded (green good, red bad, gold money, pink news)

New Production form:
- Title input (auto-suggests from pool of 24 titles)
- Lead 1 & Lead 2 dropdowns (only Available actors)
- CP name input (auto-generates portmanteau: first half of name1 + second half of name2)
- Live chemistry preview bar with shared traits, warnings for unhappy actors
- Genre: Romance, Comedy, Slice of Life, School, Office
- Type: Mini Series (3 weeks), Series (4 weeks), Movie (1 week)
- Story: Original, Adaptation
- Schedule: 3 Months (12 weeks, q0.8), 6 Months (24 weeks, q1.0), 12 Months (48 weeks, q1.3)
- Platform: TV (censored, wide reach) or Streaming (uncensored, niche, more money)
- Rating: PG, PG-13, R (TV blocks R)
- Budget: Min (x0.5), Max (x2.0), Custom slider (x0.5 to x2.5)
- Cost preview with treasury check

Production phases:
1. FILMING: progresses weekly, chemistry grows, actors become busy
2. WRAP: reveal genre×type combo (PERFECT/GOOD/BAD with multipliers 1.5/1.0/0.6), apply chemistry deltas
3. RELEASING: one episode per week, ratings 1-10, viewer counts, revenue, momentum system, fan reviews, social posts
4. FINAL CRITIQUE: four evaluators score the show (see Prompt 5)

MOBILE: Form fields stack vertically. Segmented buttons become full-width rows. Chemistry preview is collapsible.
